Figure 2. Observed-scale fitting and forecast trajectories for the completed original Korean, Japanese, and Pakistani peak origins. Black points are observations; blue EnKF and orange EnKPF lines show training fits (solid) and forecasts (dashed), with the vertical dashed line marking the final training date. Shading is each method's central 95% empirical latent ensemble interval during the held-out forecast period only; training fits are lines without shading. Future measurement noise is excluded. For readable fitting comparisons, each linear axis spans zero to 1.2 times the maximum observed count. EnKF forecast means in the Japanese and Pakistani panels and interval tails in several panels extend above these limits. Blue upward triangles mark the first cached EnKF forecast date outside the displayed range. Figure S7 shows the complete means, intervals, and member ranges without clipping. Both filters use 200 members; random-number settings are documented in the supplementary methods, and no realization was selected according to forecast error. Pakistan's LSM initializer reached its evaluation budget (exit flag 0), although its forecast completed; the converged-initializer subset is reported separately.

Figure S7. Complete ranges of the same original Korean, Japanese, and Pakistani peak-origin EnKF and EnKPF fitting and forecast trajectories shown in Figure 2. Symmetric logarithmic axes, with a linear threshold of 100 counts, include all observations and method means, along with the complete held-out latent 95% intervals and ensemble minima and maxima. Thin dotted curves show method-specific forecast-member ranges; colored shading shows central 95% latent forecast intervals. Solid lines show training fits without shading or member envelopes, dashed lines show forecasts, and vertical gray lines mark the final training dates. No means, interval endpoints, or member extrema are clipped or repaired. The latent ensemble summaries exclude future measurement noise. Pakistan's training initializer has exit flag 0 after reaching the evaluation budget; this completed forecast is retained with that limitation disclosed.
